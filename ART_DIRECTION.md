# Warm Geometry

CatSVG treats geometry as character rather than machinery. Curves should feel cut from coloured paper: economical, bold, and gently imperfect in spirit while remaining mathematically clean. Every silhouette must read before any face or accessory is added, and every named pose must communicate its action at a glance. The work should feel meticulously crafted, with attachment points and overlaps refined until no part appears pasted on by accident.

Colour behaves like a small printmaker's palette. Ink gives structure, paper creates breathing room, and one bright accent carries the wit. Markings are descriptive shapes rather than surface noise; their rhythm should strengthen the body underneath. Even maximal combinations need a clear hierarchy, the result of painstaking calibration rather than accumulation.

Faces use tiny changes to create large shifts in temperament. Eyes, nose, mouth, whiskers, and markings share one balanced facial field, with asymmetry used only as an intentional expression—not as missing anatomy. Accessories support the character without obscuring it. This calls for master-level restraint: each detail earns its place, remains legible at favicon scale, and still rewards inspection when enlarged.

Composition follows the logic of a small portrait studio. The cat owns the centre, the scene supports it, grounded props stay on the floor, and mood marks orbit without collision. Scale, posture, and unusual body shapes must preserve those relationships through explicit anchors. The final image should look like countless hours were spent making every possible combination feel authored.

The interface extends the same movement into a tactile workbench: warm paper, dark rules, concise labels, and small editorial annotations. Text stays sparse and functional while grouping reveals the visual system beneath the generator. Every spacing decision, focus state, and responsive transition should carry the same painstaking attention as the SVG itself.
